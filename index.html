About Varq

"Nothing leaves the page." Varq is a privacy-first document manager and AI assistant that runs entirely on your device — no account, no cloud, no compromise.

Local-first On-device AI Encrypted vault Independently built
What it does

A document manager that keeps everything on your phone

Varq brings together a secure vault, a full document toolkit, and a genuinely useful AI assistant — without asking you to hand your files to a server first.

🔒

Encrypted Vault

AES-256-GCM encryption for your most sensitive files, locked behind biometrics or a PIN.

📄

Full document toolkit

View, edit, annotate, and convert PDFs, Word, PowerPoint, and Excel files — all on-device.

🧠

On-device AI

Chat with your documents, get instant summaries, and search your library by meaning — powered by a language model that never leaves your phone.

🏷️

Smart organization

Automatic tagging, deadline & obligation tracking, and connections between related documents — all found locally.

✂️

Smart Redact

AI-assisted redaction that actually removes sensitive text, not just paints a black box over it.

🔍

Semantic search

Find what you mean, not just what you typed, across every document and note in your library.

Why local-first

Your files never need to leave your phone

Most document and AI apps ask you to upload your files to their servers to get anything smart done with them. Varq doesn't, because it doesn't have to — modern phones are powerful enough to run real AI on-device. That means your tax documents, medical records, and private notes stay on your device, whether or not you're online.

Read the full Privacy Policy for exactly what data Varq touches and why, and the Terms of Use for the rules on using Varq and its AI.

Who's behind it

Built independently

Varq is designed and built by a solo developer, Rohit Tiwari. It started from a simple frustration: every "smart" document app wanted cloud access to files that never needed to leave the phone in the first place. Varq is the alternative.
