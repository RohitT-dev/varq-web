01 Overview

What Varq is, and isn't

Varq — "Nothing leaves the page" — is a local-first document manager. Your files, notes, vault contents, and AI conversations are stored on your device and processed by an AI model that also runs on your device. We don't operate a server that stores, or even sees, your content, because there isn't one.

This policy covers the limited information the app does touch, the permissions it asks for and why, and how you stay in control of your data.

02 What we don't collect

No account, no analytics, no tracking

Varq does not ask you to create an account, and does not collect, transmit, or store your documents, notes, vault contents, or AI chat history anywhere outside your device.

As of the effective date below, the app includes no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software of any kind — nothing tracks how you use the app, and no usage data is sent anywhere. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.

03 Storage

How your data is stored

Everything you create or import in Varq — documents, notes, chat history, search index — is stored in a local database on your device. Anything you add to the Vault is additionally encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, a strong, industry-standard authenticated encryption method.

The encryption key is generated on your device and held in your device's secure, hardware-backed storage — never in the cloud. We keep no copy of your encryption key and have no way to decrypt your Vault, even if we wanted to.

04 Artificial intelligence

On-device AI

Varq's AI features — chat, document analysis, semantic search, smart tagging, insights — run on a language model (Gemma, via Google's LiteRT runtime) that is downloaded once and then executes entirely on your device.

When you ask the AI a question or open a document for analysis, none of that text, and none of the model's response, is sent to any server. The only network activity involved is the one-time download of the model files themselves (see "Third-party services" below) — after that, your device does the thinking, offline if you like.

06 Sharing

Data sharing

We don't sell, rent, or share your data with third parties — we can't, because we never receive it. Varq contains no advertising software and no data-broker integrations.

07 Retention

Data retention & deletion

You control retention entirely: delete a document, note, or Vault item at any time from within the app, and it's gone from your device. Uninstalling Varq removes all of its local data along with it.

Your Vault and app-lock credentials are deliberately excluded from your device's automatic cloud backup, so they won't reappear if you restore a device backup onto a new phone. Because everything lives only on your device, we strongly recommend keeping your own backups of anything important to you — if a device is lost, damaged, or reset, we have no copy to recover it from.

08 Security

Security measures

The Vault is encrypted with AES-256-GCM, an authenticated encryption standard that also protects against tampering, not just reading. App Lock uses biometric authentication and/or a PIN — your PIN is never stored in plain text; it's cryptographically hashed and salted (PBKDF2-SHA256) and protected against repeated guessing with an exponential lockout.

Sensitive screens are protected from screenshots and screen recording at the operating-system level. Varq also includes on-device checks that can detect signs of a compromised device — such as rooting, jailbreaking, or tampering — and will flag them to you.

09 Children

Children's privacy

Varq is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children — in practice, since the app collects no personal information from anyone, this is true by construction. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us using the details below.

10 Your rights

Your rights & choices

Many privacy laws (like the GDPR and CCPA) give you rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Because Varq never collects or stores your data anywhere but your own device, you already have complete, direct control over all of it — there's no request process, because there's no separate copy for us to act on. Opening the app and editing or deleting something is the whole process.

11 Third parties

Third-party services

Varq's only outbound network requests are to download its on-device AI model files, one time, from Google's and Hugging Face's official model hosting — after that download, the app's AI features run fully offline. Those providers may see standard connection information (such as your IP address) as part of serving that download, governed by their own privacy practices.

If you installed Varq from Google Play or another app store, that platform separately collects its own installation and purchase information under its own privacy policy — Varq has no visibility into, or control over, that.

12 Changes

Changes to this policy

If we make a meaningful change to how Varq handles data, we'll update this page and note the new effective date below. Since the app has no account or push channel tied to you personally, there's no way for it to notify you directly — we'd encourage checking back here occasionally, especially after an app update.
